# Lifecycle lanes

Eight mechanical stage cards group projects by the recorded lifecycle state. Each shows a textual stage, semantic indicator lamp, count, and member projects. Roadmap percentages do not move a project out of an explicit beta/live/maintenance/paused/archived stage.

Selecting a stage filters the comparison table; selecting it again removes that stage filter. Search remains active. Empty stages stay visible with zero projects, and **Roadmap complete** is explicitly separate from production readiness. Responsive columns and text ensure stage information does not depend on color or animation.
